About Vionic Systems

Practical engineering support and reliable industrial sourcing.

Engineering Solutions for Industry

Vionic Systems is a growing industrial solutions company based in Pune, India. We focus on sourcing and supplying industrial electrical products, automation components, robotics and machine vision products.

We aim to support manufacturers, machine builders, system integrators and industrial businesses with quality products, competitive pricing and responsive technical assistance.

Our approach combines product sourcing with practical engineering knowledge to help customers identify suitable components for their applications.

Meet the Founder

Hatim Gulzar Sadliwala | Founder

Hatim holds a Master's degree in Mechatronics from Germany and a Bachelor's degree in Mechatronics Engineering from India.

His academic and technical experience has developed a strong interest in industrial automation, robotics, machine vision and intelligent manufacturing.

Through Vionic Systems, he aims to make practical and affordable automation solutions more accessible to Indian industries.
