Who We Are

Practical Solutions. Reliable Connections.

Vionic Systems is a growing industrial solutions company focused on the trading and supply of industrial electrical products, robotics and automation components, sensors, and industrial safety systems.

We help industries source quality products through reliable supplier networks, practical technical support, timely response, and value-driven service.

Beyond component supply, we offer additional technical assistance, including installation support, panel wiring, sensor fitting, component replacement, troubleshooting, and maintenance support.

As we expand our engineering capabilities, we are working toward delivering advanced industrial automation solutions across machine vision, smart warehousing, robotics, and intelligent inspection.

Leadership & Vision

Meet the Founder

Hatim Gulzar Sadliwala

M.Sc. Mechatronics | Germany

Hatim Gulzar Sadliwala is the Founder of Vionic Systems and a Mechatronics Engineer with a Master's degree in Mechatronics from Germany.

His academic and technical experience in Germany developed a strong interest in industrial automation, robotics, and machine vision. This expertise led to the establishment of Vionic Systems, focused on delivering reliable automation solutions for Indian industries.

His vision is to build Vionic Systems into a trusted provider of practical and affordable automation solutions. He aims to support the growth of India's manufacturing and logistics sectors by making advanced technologies such as machine vision, robotics, warehouse automation, and intelligent material handling more accessible to businesses of all sizes.
