About Vionic Systems

Connecting Industries with Reliable Technology

Vionic Systems is an industrial automation and engineering solutions business based in Pune, Maharashtra. We focus on the sourcing and supply of industrial electrical products, automation components, sensors and robotics-related equipment.

We work with a network of suppliers to help customers procure the components they need at competitive prices. Whether it is a standard electrical component or a difficult-to-source automation product, we aim to make procurement simpler and more convenient.

Alongside product supply, we are developing our engineering capabilities in panel wiring, industrial automation, machine vision, robotics and intelligent inspection systems.

  • Competitive product sourcing
