Who We Are

About Vionic Systems

Engineering knowledge, dependable sourcing and practical industrial support.

Your Partner in Industrial Automation

Vionic Systems is a Pune-based industrial solutions company focused on supplying industrial electrical products, automation components, robotics and machine vision solutions.

We aim to support manufacturers, machine builders and system integrators by sourcing quality products and providing practical technical assistance for their industrial requirements.

Our approach is built around understanding customer requirements, identifying suitable products and delivering value-driven solutions.

Our Mission

To make reliable and practical industrial automation technologies more accessible to Indian industries.

Our Founder

Hatim Gulzar Sadliwala

Founder – Vionic Systems | M.Sc. Mechatronics

Hatim holds a Master's degree in Mechatronics from Germany and a Bachelor's degree in Mechatronics from India. His academic and technical experience developed his interest in industrial automation, robotics, machine vision and intelligent manufacturing.

Through Vionic Systems, he aims to bring practical, affordable and reliable automation solutions to Indian industries.
